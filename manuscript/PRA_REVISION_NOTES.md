# PRA Regular Article revision

## Main editorial change

The paper now opens with the quantum measurement problem: a local lossy two-level probe both reads out and loads a periodically driven finite chain. The physical conclusion is the common-preparation boundary contrast, qualified by drive-dependent line shapes. A frozen eight-drive N=4-to-N=6 raw-weight transfer is a separate finite-size result. Coupling and damping scans then explain why the probe is active.

This is a platform-independent finite-system theory/numerics paper. It neither derives circuit-QED parameters nor claims a universal topological measurement or thermodynamic time crystal. The main text avoids internal Gate labels; the Supplemental Material retains them for exact provenance.
